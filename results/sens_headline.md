## Reading

Day-of-surgery operative time and the discharge-time readmission model are labelled secondary and are never part of the primary models. The multiple-imputation analysis imputes predictors only (outcome excluded) on the whole analytic cohort before nested CV, so test-fold covariates (not outcomes) inform the imputation; this is a documented departure from fully fold-internal preprocessing.

Multiple-imputation and no-indicator arms: delta AUROC versus the primary is within 0.0012 for non-home and LOS; for readmission lr, ridge-lasso and no-indicator xgb it is within 0.0025; the maximum deviation overall is readmission xgb under multiple imputation (-0.006, 95% CI -0.013 to 0.002). All 95% intervals include 0 (sens_all.csv, delta_auroc_vs_primary).
